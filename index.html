Lumen Theater

IPTV, NAS & Video Player for Meta Quest

Lumen Theater turns your headset into a private cinema for the videos you already have: your own files, your network shares and your IPTV playlists, on a giant, curved virtual screen.

Features

  • IPTV playlists (M3U / M3U8 and Xtream-style accounts) with categories, search and favorites
  • Play files from your NAS or a Windows shared folder (SMB 2/3) without copying them
  • Videos stored on your headset, with thumbnails and resume where you left off
  • A classic cinema, a grand theater, a drive-in at dusk, a rooftop cinema, stadiums, a winter cabin and more
  • Lights that dim while you watch, and ambient light that glows with the colors of your film
  • Control with controllers or your hands
  • English, Turkish, German, Spanish and French

Lumen Theater does not include any channels, playlists or other content. Use it only with content you have the right to access.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to sevingames.dev@gmail.com. Please include your headset model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

A playlist does not load? Check the address in a web browser first. The app shows the server's error message under the list. Lists from a file can also be copied to the headset over USB.
